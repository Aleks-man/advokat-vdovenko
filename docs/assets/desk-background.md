## Промпт

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide photographic background for a refined Russian criminal defense lawyer website, not a website mockup. Create a photorealistic overhead editorial still life on an expansive pale warm limestone / ivory desk. An open substantial law reference book with ivory pages is partly cropped at the far upper-right edge, its spine diagonal and pages naturally curved; a slim elegant dark fountain pen with a small muted brass detail rests near it; a subtle corner of a plain pale taupe document folder enters at the far lower-left edge. Keep the central 70 percent mostly empty pale desk, especially center and lower center. Objects only around the outer edges, breathing room, asymmetrical tasteful composition. Very high-key soft natural window light, soft long shadows, low saturation, quiet sophisticated real materials, subtle realistic limestone and paper texture. Palette warm off-white #f3f1eb, pale greige, tiny muted brass details, no large dark surfaces. Objects recognizable yet light and low contrast, suitable under an additional ivory translucent overlay. Landscape 3:2 composition. No people, no hands, no coffee, no plants, no gavel, no scales, no logos, no watermark, no readable book titles or readable text; page printing may be faint out-of-focus typographic lines only. Do not depict any webpage, buttons or interface.
